## Section 7: FAQ

**Have Questions About Solar? Our FAQ may be the answer:**

**Q: What are the benefits of going solar?**
There are many benefits of going solar. For the purposes of this FAQ, we'll stick to three:
- You're guaranteed a cheaper energy bill all year round.
- Immunity from price hikes. Most power providers raise their rates 8–10% every year.
- Reduction of greenhouse gas emissions and fossil fuel burning.

**Q: How much is my solar system going to cost?**
This depends on how big your system is. However, most systems range anywhere from $35,000–$50,000. If you'd like to know what your system will cost you, get a quote!

**Q: How long does the install take?**
On average, it will take about 90 days from contract signing to activation. However, we're working hard toward a company-wide goal of a 45-day install.

**Q: How much can I save each month?**
Again, this depends on where you live and what your current usage is. However, our customers save around $1,500 in their first year alone.

**Q: Do I get tax incentives?**
Most likely! It can vary state to state, but most state governments offer incentives like tax credits and rebates (as much as 30% of the cost of your system). We can give you a more accurate estimate.

**Q: How long does it take for solar panels to pay for themselves?**
Depending on where you live, it can take anywhere from 6–10 years for solar panels to pay for themselves. In the meantime, you'll be saving on your overall energy bill every single month.
