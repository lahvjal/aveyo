## State Sections

### Illinois

Illinois residents experience some of the highest electricity rates in the Midwest, making solar one of the smartest financial decisions a homeowner can make. Illinois also offers strong state-level incentive programs — including the Illinois Shines (Adjustable Block Program) — that can significantly lower the cost of going solar. Aveyo helps Illinois homeowners take full advantage of available rebates and tax credits while locking in predictable monthly energy payments and reducing reliance on the aging grid.

### Pennsylvania

Pennsylvania homeowners are no strangers to rising utility bills, and solar offers a reliable path to more predictable monthly energy costs. Aveyo's local advisors understand Pennsylvania's energy landscape and will walk you through every available incentive — from the federal ITC to Pennsylvania's own Alternative Energy Portfolio Standards programs.

### California

California residents face some of the highest electricity rates in the nation, and with the state's ambitious clean energy goals, solar is no longer just a smart choice — it's becoming the standard. California's NEM 3.0 policy makes pairing solar with battery storage more valuable than ever, letting you store the energy you generate and use it when rates are highest. Aveyo's California team will help you design a system that maximizes your savings and keeps your home powered through outages and peak-rate hours alike.

## FAQ Section

**Q: Does my state qualify for solar with Aveyo?**
A: Aveyo currently serves homeowners in Illinois, Utah, Pennsylvania, and California. Each of these states has unique incentives, net metering policies, and solar potential that our advisors are experts in. Get a quote to find out exactly what your home qualifies for.

**Q: Where is Aveyo located?**
A: Aveyo is headquartered in American Fork, Utah, and serves homeowners across Illinois, Utah, Pennsylvania, and California. Our local teams are embedded in the communities we serve, so you'll always work with someone who knows your area.

**Q: What solar incentives am I eligible for in my state?**
A: Every state has its own set of incentives, rebates, and tax credits for going solar. On top of the federal Solar Investment Tax Credit (ITC), states like Illinois, Pennsylvania, and California offer additional programs. Our advisors will identify every dollar you're entitled to. You can also explore incentives through DSIRE (dsireusa.org).

**Q: What plans does Aveyo offer?**
A: Aveyo offers two primary plans: the **Energy Subscription Plan** (lease your panels, enjoy predictable payments) and the **Energy Purchase Plan** (own your system outright and maximize long-term savings). Both plans include our Stress-Free Guarantee and a 25-year equipment warranty.
