## Section: 07 Steps to Solar Savings

**Heading:** Here's what it looks like:

### 01) SITE SURVEY
We check your roof, attic, electrical infrastructure, and shading to make sure your home is ready for solar.

### 02) CAD/ENGINEERING
We design a high-yield, low-panel system to increase your offset and decrease your cost.

### 03) PERMITS SENT / PERMITS APPROVED
Paperwork is sent to the city for approval.

### 04) THE INSTALL
1–2 days (depending on system size) is all we need to install your panels.

### 05) THE INSPECTION
An inspector checks to ensure your system meets your high standards.

### 06) NET METER INSTALLATION
A device that tracks your offset by measuring solar production and home energy output.

### 07) ACTIVATION
It's time to turn your system on! Let the offset begin.
